# Company Remote Work Policy (Effective January 1, 2025)

## 1. Introduction
Our company is committed to fostering a flexible work environment that supports employee well-being and productivity. This policy outlines the guidelines for remote and hybrid work arrangements.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees are eligible for remote work if their role can be effectively performed outside the office and with manager approval. Certain roles, requiring on-site presence, may not be eligible. Employees must reside in a country where the company has a legal entity.

## 3. Remote Work Agreement
All remote employees must sign a Remote Work Agreement form, which details responsibilities, work hours, communication expectations, and equipment provisions. This agreement is reviewed annually.

## 4. Work Environment & Equipment
The company provides a stipend for essential office equipment. Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and productive remote workspace. Internet connectivity is the employee's responsibility.

## 5. Communication & Collaboration
Remote employees are expected to maintain regular communication with their teams and managers through company-approved channels (e.g., Slack, Google Meet, email). Participation in team meetings and relevant company events is mandatory.

## 6. Security
All company data accessed remotely must adhere to our stringent data security protocols. Use of personal devices for company data is strictly prohibited unless explicitly approved and secured.

## 7. Performance & Review
Performance evaluations for remote employees will be based on objectives and outcomes, consistent with those for in-office employees. Managers will provide regular feedback and support.

## 8. International Remote Work
Short-term international remote work (up to 30 days per year) requires pre-approval from HR and management due to tax and compliance implications. Long-term international remote work is generally not supported unless approved by senior leadership and legal counsel.
